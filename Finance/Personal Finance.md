# Credit Score
It is a score that signals our creditworthiness. That is how likely we are to repay our borrowed money on time. In India it is called the CIBIL Score and it ranges from 300 - 900.
- Excellent: 750 - 900
- Good: 700 - 749
- Fair: 600 - 699
- Poor: 300 - 599

*Why it matters?* 
- The higher our credit score the faster/easier our loans are approved.
- People with higher credit scores also have lower interest rates.
- Access to premium credit cards.

*How it is calculated?*
- Payment History (30%): Do we pay our EMIs and credit card bills on time.
- Credit Utilization Ratio (25%): How much of our available credit limit are we using. Best to keep it under 30%
- Credit Age and History (25%): The length of our credit history.
- Recent Inquiries (20%): Every time you apply for new credit, a hard inquiry is logged. Applying for multiple credit lines in a short period can lower your score
