#finance 
### Quantitative Tightening
Where the central banks will sell a bunch of [[Bonds|government bonds]] to take money out of the economy and store it at the central bank. Therefore reducing cashflow and slowing down inflation.

### Quantitative Easing
Where the central banks will buy a bunch of [[Bonds|government bonds]] to give more money back into the economy.
