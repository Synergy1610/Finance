#finance 
# Simple Interest
Interest is earned only on our principal amount.

*Lets say 1000rs at 8%*: This means 80rs every year on the same 1000rs.

# Compound Interest
Interest is earned on our original amount *plus* all our previously earned interest.

*Consider the same 1000rs at 8%*
Year 1: 1000rs + 80rs = 1080rs
Year 2: 1080rs + 86rs = 1166rs
Year 3: 1166rs + 93rs = 1259rs

So basically our intrest starts earning its own interest.

### Why it matters?
₹1,000 at 8% simple for 30 years = ₹3,400 *BUT*
₹1,000 at 8% compound for 30 years = ₹10,000+

Same rate. Same amount. Just compounding makes that difference.

**KEY VARIABLES:** 
- Rate: Higher the % = Faster growth
- Time: The most powerful factor
- Frequency: Compounded monthly grows more than compounded annually
