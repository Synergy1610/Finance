#finance 

Reference: [Varsity Page](https://zerodha.com/varsity/chapter/the-need-to-invest/)

There are many different things that we can invest in, and they all are mainly divided into **4 groups**:

### Fixed Income Instruments
The returns are fixed as the rates are predetermined.
Examples: [[Bonds]], Fixed Deposits

### Equity
Equity means ownership. 
When you buy shares/stocks of a publicily listed company, we basically own a piece of the company giving us ownership, thats why *stocks are called equities*.

In India the shares are mainly traded in either the *Bombay Stock Exchange* (BSE) or *Naitonal Stock Exchange* (NSE)

### Real Estate
Involves buying and selling of Commercial and Non-commercial lands/plots/ apartments etc.

2 Main ways to make profits:
 - Rent
 - Increase in land value (Capital Appreciation)

*Capital Appreciation:* Increase in the value of an asset over time.

### Commodities
Involves investing in Gold and Silver. 
Either in jewellery, ETF's (Exchange Traded Funds) or Soverign Gold Bonds.
