# What is a bond?
It is basically a type of loan. Where the *buyer is the lender* and the *issuer is the borrower*.

So when the govt/corporation needs money, they issue a bond, which people can buy thus giving the issuer's their money.
And the issuer promises to 
- Pay the lender a fixed interest (called *Coupon*) periodically.
- And return the original amount (called *Principal*) at the end of the agreed period (called *Maturity*).

**Interest type:** Bonds pay simple interest — fixed coupon on the original amount every period. If you reinvest those coupons, the overall effect becomes compounding.

### Government Bonds
When the government needs more money than the collected taxes, they issue bonds which people can buy.
Very safe, lower interest. The government uses this for pulic spending.

### Corporate Bonds
Similar to govt bonds, but when a corporation needs money. Riskier than govt bonds, because the company could go bankrupt and not pay you back. This is why they offer higher interest rates to attract investors.
*The riskier the company, the higher the interest.*
